# Hero

**Species:** Guinea pig  
**Status:** Sanctuary resident  
**Condition:** Senior care, ongoing dental monitoring

---

Hero is one of HALT Rescue's most beloved long-term residents. He has been with us for years and shows no signs of slowing down. He is the patriarch of the big herd and father of Gothic, and he has a personality that fills the room.

## His Personality

Hero is a talker. He has a habit of "blurping" — his own form of vocalizing — and he always has a lot to say, especially when he wants attention. He loves having his backside rubbed and is an enthusiastic snugglebug. He once watched an entire baseball game curled up in Alanna's lap.

## His Health

Hero receives regular dental check-ups to monitor his molars. His teeth are watched closely as part of his routine care, and he gets periodic peeks from Dr. Dana to make sure everything is wearing evenly.

He lives with the big herd alongside his son Gothic and several other long-term residents.
