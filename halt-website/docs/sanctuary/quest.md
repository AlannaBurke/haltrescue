# Quest

**Species:** Guinea pig  
**Status:** Sanctuary resident  
**Condition:** Ongoing health management

---

Quest is one of HALT Rescue's long-term residents with a rich history at the sanctuary. He came in alongside Quill, and the two were bonded for a time — but as Quest's health changed, their relationship fell apart. This is not uncommon in guinea pigs; it can be a survival instinct when one animal senses illness in another.

## His Story

Quest has been with HALT for years and has required ongoing veterinary attention for various health concerns. Despite his challenges, he remains a familiar and cherished face in the sanctuary.

## Bonding and Social Dynamics

Quest's story is a good reminder that guinea pig bonds can change over time, especially when health is a factor. If you notice a previously bonded pair starting to fight or separate, it is worth consulting a vet to rule out underlying illness in one or both animals.
