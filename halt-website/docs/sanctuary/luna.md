# Luna

**Species:** Rat  
**Status:** Sanctuary resident  
**Condition:** No hind legs; exceptional mobility

---

Luna is proof that animals adapt in ways that constantly amaze us. She has no hind legs, and yet she navigates her world with confidence and curiosity. She has been known to climb onto the recliner, steal hair ties, and generally make herself at home wherever she pleases.

## Her Story

Luna came to HALT from the same situation as Tilly, a HALT alumni who has since been adopted. Despite her physical limitations, Luna has never let her missing legs slow her down. She has developed her own techniques for getting around and continues to surprise her caregivers with what she can accomplish.

## Her Care

Luna's care focuses on keeping her environment accessible and safe. Ramps, soft bedding, and thoughtful cage design help her move freely without putting strain on her front limbs. She is monitored for any signs of overuse injury or skin irritation from dragging.

## What Luna Teaches Us

Luna is a reminder that "special needs" does not mean "lower quality of life." With the right setup and attentive care, animals with significant physical differences can thrive.
