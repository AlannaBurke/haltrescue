# Gothic

**Species:** Guinea pig  
**Status:** Sanctuary resident  
**Condition:** Lymph node inflammation under monitoring

---

Gothic is the son of Hero, one of HALT's most beloved long-term residents. He lives with the big herd alongside his dad and several other sanctuary animals.

Gothic is currently being monitored for inflamed lymph nodes. His care team is keeping a close eye on him and he has been seen by Dr. Dana as part of a recent batch of vet visits.

His page will be updated as his story develops.
