## Water Bottles and Sibling Rivalry

The most exciting development this week: the precocious ones have learned to use the water bottle. They have also learned to fight over the water bottle, which, honestly, is a more essential rat life skill. They will spend far more of their adult lives fighting over water bottles than actually drinking from them. I consider this a developmental milestone.

## The Adult Rats Are Testing Me

The adult rats have also been testing my patience this week. They have developed a strong preference for the two smaller, more annoying-to-fill water bottles over the three large, easy-to-fill bottles I have provided for their convenience.

It is as if they know. They want to make more work for me. I respect the commitment to chaos.

## The Cuteness Carries Us

Their overwhelming cuteness is genuinely what is getting me through the logistical challenges of caring for this many rats at once. I just want to snuggle them all day. I hope we can raise them all to be big, healthy, happy rats who go on to wonderful homes.
