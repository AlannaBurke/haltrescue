# Hero

**Species:** Guinea pig  
**Status:** Sanctuary resident  
**Genetics:** Lethal white  
**Veterinarian:** Dr. Ford

---

Hero is one of HALT Rescue's most beloved residents. A lethal white guinea pig, he is approaching his first birthday and has a personality that fills the room.

## His Family

Hero's dad is **Gothic**, who lives in his own herd here at HALT. His mom is **Rhyme**, who also lives in the big herd at HALT. His brother **Ziggy**, who does not have health issues, lives with HALT board member Alanna. His brother **Caper** sadly passed away at just a few months old.

## What Is a Lethal White?

Lethal white guinea pigs are born when two guinea pigs carrying the roan or dalmation gene are bred together. The combination can result in offspring with white coats, small or absent eyes, and underdeveloped teeth and digestive systems. Many lethal whites require extra veterinary care and hand-feeding, and some do not survive. Hero is a fighter.

## His Personality

Hero is a talker. He vocalizes constantly and always has something to say, especially when he wants attention. He loves having his backside rubbed and is an enthusiastic snugglebug. He watches Phillies games with Danni and takes the games very seriously.

## His Health

As a lethal white, Hero's teeth and overall health are monitored closely by **Dr. Ford**. Lethal whites are prone to dental issues due to their genetics, so routine check-ups are an important part of his care.
